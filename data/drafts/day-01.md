Can AI replace a QA engineer? Short answer: no. But it is changing how we work.

Over my four years in testing, from automotive systems to enterprise software, I have learned one core truth. **Testing is not just execution.** It is about critical thinking, asking the right questions, and finding the gaps that developers missed.

Imagine an AI tool generating fifty test cases for a new login screen in seconds. It covers the happy path and standard edge cases quickly. But does it check how the system behaves when the network drops mid-transaction while an authentication token expires? That needs human test design.

From a QA perspective, **testing skills transfer directly to AI**. The ability to break things on purpose, analyze vague requirements, and spot logical inconsistencies is more valuable than ever. AI handles the heavy lifting of generation, but we provide the boundary conditions and the risk analysis.

One thing I've learned while exploring AI for testing is that tools are only as smart as the person prompting and verifying them.
